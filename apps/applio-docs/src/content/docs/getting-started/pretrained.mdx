A **training pretrained** supplies starting weights for a new experiment. It is different from an exported [inference voice model](/getting-started/installing-inference-models/).

- **Generator (G):** synthesizes audio during training.
- **Discriminator (D):** evaluates generated and real audio during training.

Use a compatible G/D pair. The trained generator is exported into an inference model; the discriminator is not used for conversion.

## Match the training configuration

Check the publisher's architecture, sample rate, pitch-guidance configuration, and other compatibility requirements. A 48 kHz pretrained pair cannot simply be selected for a 40 kHz experiment. Applio-specific vocoders may need their own pretrainings.

## Download and select a pair

<Steps>

1. In Desktop, open **Models → Download Models → Pretrained Base Models**. In Gradio, use the pretrained downloader in **Download**.
2. Choose an available pair and its sample rate, or supply the publisher's G and D download links.
3. Wait for both downloads to finish.
4. Open **Training** and select compatible architecture and sampling-rate settings.
5. Enable pretrained initialization. For a custom pair, enable **Custom Pretrained** and select both G and D paths.
6. Check the training log confirms that the expected weights were loaded.

</Steps>

You can also upload local G/D files through Desktop's training controls. The downloadable catalog may change; use the current in-app list and the publisher's model card rather than choosing by name alone.

## Continue training or start a new voice?

A pretrained pair initializes a new run. Resuming an existing experiment uses that experiment's checkpoints and configuration. See [resume training](/getting-started/training/#resume-training).

Training without pretrainings is available for advanced experiments, but needs its own dataset and training plan. There is no fixed audio duration or epoch count that guarantees a good base model.

<Aside type="note">
  Back up custom G/D files and record where they came from. Downloading an inference model does not provide the discriminator or optimizer state for resuming its original training.
</Aside>
