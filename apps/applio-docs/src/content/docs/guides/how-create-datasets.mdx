A useful dataset represents the intended voice clearly across the sounds and delivery you want the model to handle. Keep original recordings and perform cleanup on copies.

## Select recordings

Choose recordings you have permission to use. Prefer a consistent speaker, little background noise or reverberation, and natural variation in pitch, articulation, and emotion.

There is no fixed duration that guarantees quality. A smaller clean and varied dataset can be more useful than many repetitive or damaged recordings. Avoid duplicates and long passages with no useful voice.

For singing models, include relevant pitch ranges and delivery. For speech models, include varied phrases and speaking styles. Separate speakers into numeric folders for multispeaker training.

## Isolate and clean

<Steps>

1. If music is present, [separate the vocal](/guides/uvr/) and listen for remaining instruments and separation artifacts.
2. Remove wrong-speaker sections, strong interference, and unusable recordings.
3. Apply noise reduction only where necessary. Compare before and after to avoid removing consonants or vocal detail.
4. Trim long silent edges while retaining natural word boundaries.
5. Balance levels and leave peak headroom. Lowering a clipped recording's level does not undo clipping.

</Steps>

Avoid strong gating, compression, denoising, or reverb removal simply because they are available. Natural breaths and unvoiced consonants can be useful training material.

## Review in Desktop

In **Training**, use dataset review to scan recordings and inspect flagged sections. Listen to clips before excluding them. **Prepare training copy** can trim silent edges, balance recording levels, and lower full-scale peaks while keeping the source dataset separate.

Select **Use this dataset for training** when the prepared copy is ready.

## Export and organize

Use lossless WAV or FLAC where possible. Converting a lossy source into WAV does not restore lost information. Training preprocessing resamples input files to the selected model rate; that rate must match the architecture and pretrainings.

~~~text
assets/datasets/MyVoice/
  recording-one.wav
  recording-two.flac
~~~

For multispeaker training:

~~~text
assets/datasets/MyVoice/
  0/
    first-speaker.wav
  1/
    second-speaker.wav
~~~

Desktop can upload files or use a host-side dataset path. Ensure the selected method preserves speaker folders when needed. Gradio commonly uses folders under `assets/datasets`.

<Aside type="tip">
  Hold back a few recordings for evaluation. Use the same held-out clips to compare checkpoints so that training progress is judged on audio the model did not train on.
</Aside>
